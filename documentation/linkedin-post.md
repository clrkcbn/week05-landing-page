Week 5 – Client-Server Technologies

This week I designed and built a **Responsive Product Landing Page** with **Laravel, Blade Components, and Tailwind CSS**.

The brief: take a real neighbourhood business and give it a clean, mobile-first site. I built one for a
grooming studio, "Fadehouse Barber Co.", with a navbar, hero, six-feature grid, product showcase,
three membership plans with a monthly/annual switch, testimonials, a booking form with inline
validation, a closing call-to-action, and a footer.

What I practised:

- **Reusable Blade components** — 12 components (button, icon, cards, navbar, footer, …). Each section
  is a short list of `<x-…>` tags instead of copy-pasted HTML, so one fix updates every instance.
- **Tailwind CSS** — utility-first styling with a small set of design tokens (one accent colour, one
  type scale, one spacing rhythm, one radius system) and responsive prefixes for every breakpoint.
- **Responsive design** — mobile-first, tested at 390 / 820 / 1280 / 1440px with Flexbox and CSS Grid.
- **UI/UX details** — light/dark mode, hover/focus/active states, reduced-motion support, a skip link,
  and 4.5:1 contrast.

Comparing the unstyled markup with the finished page was a good reminder that structure comes first
and styling is what turns a stack of text into something people can actually scan and act on.
